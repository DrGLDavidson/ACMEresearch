I am a Laboratory Technician in the Davidson Lab, where I investigate the potential of gut microbes to act as future probiotics. My work involves conducting in vitro assays to evaluate beneficial microbial traits and exploring potential trade-offs between different host benefits. In particular, I am interested in whether microbial strains that improve chick growth and survival may also negatively influence immune development. Before joining the Davidson Lab, I completed an MRes in Medical Biology and worked in an animal diagnostic microbiology laboratory.
